Frequently Asked Questions

Understand the basics of how our financing options work before you apply.

Does applying for financing affect my credit score?

Applying for financing typically involves a credit check. The exact impact depends on the specific lender's policies. Please review the lender's terms before submitting an application.

Can I finance a repair instead of a full replacement?

Yes, financing is often available for both significant repairs and full system replacements, depending on the lender's specific criteria and the scope of the work.

How long does approval take?

Approval times vary by provider. In many cases, you can receive a decision shortly after submitting your application, allowing us to schedule service quickly.

What information do I need to apply?

Typically, you will need standard personal identification, income information, and a Social Security Number for the credit check. Our team will guide you through the required steps.
